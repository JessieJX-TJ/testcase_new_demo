状态类描述
制动灯驱动处理模块状态用以表征当前制动灯实际执行情况:
BrkLghtSts_HW=0x0=BrkLghtSts_HW_0_Off
BrkLghtFltSts_HW=0x0=BrkLghtFltSts_HW_0_False
{BrakeLight. BrakeLightState. BrakeLightSta} {InstanceID=1}=0x0=OFF
或者
BrkLghtSts_HW=0x1=BrkLghtSts_HW_1_On
{BrakeLight. BrakeLightState. BrakeLightSta} {InstanceID=1}=0x1=ON
表示当前制动灯执行状态
