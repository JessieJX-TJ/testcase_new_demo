ICB接收[远光灯开关控制模块LHZCU]远光灯控制信号,并将远光灯控制指令分发至LHZCU、RHZ
[远光灯驱动处理模块LHZCU/RHZCU]接收[远光灯开关控制模块LHZCU]控制信号,内部跳变为硬
远光灯
(exlMainBeamTypePrm=0x1=Main beam and dipped beam are one bulb；即当远光灯与近光灯为一个灯
动控制近光灯)
ICB接收控制信号:
LMainBeamLghtCtrl=0x0=LMainBeamLghtCtrl_0_Off
做退出远光灯图标指示灯显示
[远光灯驱动处理模块LHZCU/RHZCU]将远光灯执行状态反馈至[远光灯开关控制模块LHZCU]:
LMainBeamLghtSts=0x0=LMainBeamLghtSts_0_Off
RMainBeamLghtSts=0x0=RMainBeamLghtSts_0_Off
ICB将状态/故障状态反馈至[远光灯开关控制模块LHZCU]:
LMainBeamLghtFltSts=0x0=LMainBeamLghtFltSts_0_False
RMainBeamLghtFltSts=0x0=RMainBeamLghtFltSts_0_False
{MainBeamLight. MainBeamLightStatus. MainBeamLightSta}{InstanceID=1}=0x0=OFF

(exlMainBeamHwDrvEnPrm=0x0时,通过InstanceID1,2获取状态)

{MainBeamLight. MainBeamLightStatus. MainBeamLightSta}{InstanceID=4}=0x0=OFF
(exlMainBeamHwDrvEnPrm=0x1时,通过InstanceID=4获取右侧远光灯状态)

{ExteriorLightControlSystem. MainBeamLightSts. MainBeamLghtSts}=0x0=OFF

MainBeamLghtSwSts=0x0=MainBeamLghtSwSts_0_Off
